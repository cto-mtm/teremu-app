**\#\# Agentic Economy Prize**

**\*\*Are you opting into the external $50K Agentic Economy Prize?\*\***

**\> The Agentic Economy Prize is independently funded, administered, and judged by Circle. It is not a judged category of the Build with Gemini XPRIZE, and XPRIZE and Devpost play no role in evaluating submissions. Eligible participants may opt in during their Build with Gemini XPRIZE submission; full rules and judging criteria will be published by Circle.**

**To be eligible, your project must use Circle's Agent Stack to enable AI agents to autonomously make and/or receive payments. Submissions must include:**

**\* A link to a public GitHub repo verifying the integration.**  
**\* A recorded demo of the product executing at least one real, verifiable USDC transaction.**  
**\* The agent's Circle wallet address and a clickable block-explorer URL (e.g., Basescan, Arbiscan) as proof of the transaction.**
